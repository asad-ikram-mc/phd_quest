Research

Ikram, A. and Haig, E. "Is the Platform Part of the Measurement? A Protocol and Simulation Study for Cross-Platform Equivalence of Machine-Labelled Policy Sentiment." Paper, being posted to arXiv; ICWSM submission planned. Treats each platform as a survey mode and applies generalizability theory, measurement invariance and differential item functioning, and score linking to machine-labelled text. I built the simulation: with known ground truth, pooling platform scores at face value gives the wrong sign of daily aggregate sentiment on roughly a quarter of days, from measurement differences alone. Code: github.com/asadfix/platform-measurement, Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23105129.

Label validity at Artemis AI (2026). Found that two LLM labelling tiers agreed on 82.9% of sentiment labels but 0% of toxicity labels because of mismatched taxonomies, while the model reported high confidence; a September 2026 audit showed reported accuracies measured agreement with the labelling model rather than with people. Evaluation is being rebuilt around a human-labelled set. No peer-reviewed publications to date.
