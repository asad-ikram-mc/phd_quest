The problem

We pool sentiment across platforms and read the gap as opinion.

Xshort, fast, adversarial
Redditthreaded, long-form
YouTubereactions under video
Quoraexplanatory answers

That only works if a classifier measures the same construct, on the same scale, on every platform. Some studies validate platform by platform; to our knowledge none has modelled the platform as a facet of the measurement itself.

The idea

Treat each platform like a survey mode.

Survey researchers test whether a phone and an online questionnaire measure alike before comparing them. We bring that toolkit to machine-labelled text.

Generalizability theoryhow much variance is the platform?
Measurement invariance & DIFdoes the instrument behave alike?

The protocol

Six steps before you average.

  1. Same-event divergencedo platforms differ on the same event?
  2. Reliability per platforma generalizability study against human labels
  3. Invariance and DIFwith the platform as the group
  4. Timing and compositionadjust within anchor windows
  5. Score linkingthrough anchor events fixed in advance
  6. Cost of assuming equivalencequantified in simulation
