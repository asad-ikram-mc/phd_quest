Hello

Hi, I'm Asad Ikram.

My question is simple:

can we trust AI to tell us

what people really think?

I care about this because

my own AI got it wrong.

What went wrong

For my Master's, I built an AI

to read what people said about UK policy online.

Later, I found three mistakes.

One: it ignored posts in Urdu and Hindi.

Two: it treated every platform the same.

Three: it looked right,

but it was only agreeing with itself.

I see the same problem in my start-up today.

· pause ·

My research

So here is what I want to study.

If AI says people are angrier on Reddit than on X,

is that real?

Or is it the AI?

Or the platform, choosing what we see?

I wrote a paper on this with my supervisor, Dr Ella Haig.

It's a plan and a test on made-up data.

My PhD would test it on real data.

· pause ·

Three steps

First: does AI understand the same message

in Urdu, Roman Urdu and English?

Second: does it read the same news story

differently on different platforms?

Third: when an AI model is updated,

did people change, or did the AI?
