Your AI Conversational Interviewing paper found that voice and chat draw different answers out of the same people. I have spent the last two years on the same problem in social media, where every platform changes how an opinion gets written, and I would love to bring that to your Emmy Noether group as the PhD Researcher in Computational Social Science.
