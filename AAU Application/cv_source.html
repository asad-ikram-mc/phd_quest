Research

Ikram, A. and Haig, E. "Is the Platform Part of the Measurement? A Protocol and Simulation Study for Cross-Platform Equivalence of Machine-Labelled Policy Sentiment." Manuscript, complete (v1, October 2026); not yet submitted or published. Code: github.com/asadfix/platform-measurement, Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23105129. Treats each social platform as a survey mode and applies generalizability theory, measurement invariance and differential item functioning, and score linking to machine-labelled text. In a 500-replication simulation with known ground truth, an index that pools platform-level scores at face value reports the wrong sign of the aggregate on roughly a quarter of days, from measurement differences between platforms alone. No peer-reviewed publications to date.
