## 5. THE RESEARCH PROPOSAL — specification only. You write it.

Two pages. The advert sets the question for you:

> "how to examine the amount and character of negative news in news outlets and individual social
> media feeds"

**It must be anchored in SP1, use data donations and automated content analyses, and describe the
theoretical, methodological and analytical approach.**

### Questions it has to answer

**On the construct — this is where your actual contribution is:**
- What *is* "negativity" as a measured quantity, and who decides? Negativity is a machine label. Your
  entire body of work is about what happens to a finding when the label is produced by an instrument
  nobody validated. "Amount and character of negativity" **across outlets and platforms** is a
  cross-context comparability question, which is the Haig manuscript's exact machinery with
  negativity in place of sentiment.
- If negativity is scored by a classifier or an LLM, does a negativity score mean the same thing in a
  broadsheet article, a tabloid push notification, and a TikTok caption? If it does not, the
  cross-outlet comparison that SP1 is built to make inherits that.

**On the data-donation infrastructure — this is where you beat the field:**
- What does a data-donation hub in a public space actually have to do? Consent capture, platform
  export formats (Facebook DYI, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube takeout), format drift when a platform
  changes its export schema, deduplication, incomplete donations, and the attrition problem.
- **The silent-failure argument, which is yours and which won the VU letter:** a donation pipeline
  that returns a valid-looking but thin export does not break, it just under-counts. On a measure of
  *how much* negative news someone consumes, that does not read as a data-quality failure. It reads
  as a healthier news diet.
- Donation samples are self-selected. What does that do to a population estimate of news diets, and
  what would you do about it?

**On method:**
- What is the unit: the article, the post, the impression, or the person-day?
- How do you validate the negativity measure against human coding, at what sample size and what
  agreement coefficient? This group publishes in journalism studies where inter-coder reliability is
  standard and expected.
- Danish-language content and Danish-language models. You do not speak Danish. **Name it and say how
  you would handle it** — that is the Agder Section 9 move and it works.

**What NOT to do:** do not propose a new project. The advert says "the overall focus, structure, and
methodological approaches are given," and you "will also be able to affect the direction." Fit
inside SP1 and add the measurement layer. A candidate who redesigns their project loses.
