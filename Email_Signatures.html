Installing in Gmail

  1. Open this file in Chrome and select everything inside one white box. Start just before the name and finish just after the last link.
  2. Copy.
  3. In Gmail: gear icon, then See all settings, then General, then scroll to Signature.
  4. Create new, name it academic, and paste into the editor.
  5. Under Signature defaults, set For new emails use to academic. Add signature 4 as a second signature named reply and set On reply/forward use to it.
  6. Tick Insert signature before quoted text so it does not end up buried under the whole thread.
  7. Scroll to the bottom and press Save Changes. Gmail discards everything if you navigate away first.

Send yourself one test email and check it on your phone as well as the desktop before using it on Casas.

What I left out, and why

  • Your location. "Lahore, Pakistan" in a signature announces your fee status before the conversation has earned it. Royal Holloway turned you down on exactly that, and at Agder you raised eligibility yourself, deliberately, at the point it mattered. Keep that choice yours rather than automatic.
  • Images, logos and headshots. Remote images are blocked by default in most university mail clients, so they arrive as broken boxes, and they push the message into the promotions bucket.
  • Social icons. Same blocking problem, and they add nothing that a text link does not.
  • A quote or a mission line. In an academic inbox it reads as branding. Your first paragraph is the pitch.
  • Confidentiality disclaimers. They mean nothing on personal mail and add four lines of grey.
  • Custom fonts. Gmail replaces anything it does not recognise, so these use Arial, which renders identically everywhere.
