# Lund PA2026/2879 "A Divisive Spiral?": research behind the application (16 Oct 2026)

## The project (SweCRIS 2026-00291_VR, SEK 4,859,000, 2027-2030; PI Bäck, co-I Renström)
- WP1: affective polarisation in parliamentary debates, multimodal (text + audio).
- WP2: how citizen engagement on social media shapes representatives' communication. <- the plan targets this
- WP3: experiments on polarising elite messages and algorithmic systems.
- Sweden vs Finland (similar multiparty systems, different electoral systems). "A PhD student with data science skills will be recruited."
- Predecessor: Divid-Dem (Renström PI, Bäck co-I; politicians' social media rhetoric + engagement + experiments). Sibling: Uppsala "From Consensus to Conflict?" (Borges PI, Bäck co-I).

## Hooks used (verified from full texts by the research agent)
- Bäck, Carroll, Renström & Ryan 2023 (Electoral Studies 84:102639): neutral tweets with party cues raise AP; limitation: use valenced, real messages.
- Renström, Bäck & Carroll 2023 (Political Psychology 44(6)): threat -> anger (not fear) -> AP; recommend dyadic (party-pair) measures.
- Røed, Bäck & Carroll 2025 (Party Politics 31(1)): Storting, GloVe-induced negativity dictionary, 11,766 hits validated (>92%); calls for "alternative ways to validate measures of elite sentiment".
- Calvo 2025 thesis (supervised by Bäck): Riksdag, Lexicoder/AFINN, name-count target party; valence only; "could have utilized large language models like BERT", "would have used crowd-sourcing to validate".
- Wendsjö, Bäck & Kokkonen 2025 (JOP): male hostility spiral on Twitter.
