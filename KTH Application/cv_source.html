  • LLM labelling and distillation chain that produces the training data: Claude labels a stratified seed of about 6,000 posts together with its reasoning, the decision rules in that reasoning are mined into a prompt for DeepSeek, DeepSeek labels about 12,000 more, and any case below 0.80 confidence routes back to Claude. Weekly routing loop with three-tier confidence routing: roughly 75% auto-committed above 0.85, 18% sent to review between 0.60 and 0.85, 7% held below 0.60. Machine learning in research

Master's thesis: Cross-Platform Sentiment Analysis of Public Reaction to UK Economic Policies. Fine-tuned a BERT classifier to macro-F1 0.878 against BiLSTM and lexicon baselines, with human validation at Cohen's κ ≈ 0.78, temperature-scaling calibration of the predicted probabilities, McNemar and bootstrap significance testing of model differences, chi-square topic tests, LIME and integrated gradients for interpretability, NRC emotion analysis linking anger and fear to negative policy reactions, and Prophet forecasting of the daily sentiment index (MAE ≈ 0.02). Collected ~279,000 public posts on UK economic policy from X/Twitter, Reddit, YouTube and Quora via official APIs and licensed feeds, under ethics approval (Portsmouth TETHIC-2025-111094) and GDPR-compliant procedures. The finding that shaped my research interest was not the accuracy: the same policy event produced structurally different sentiment and emotion profiles on different platforms, which meant the cross-platform aggregate rested on an untested assumption, that the classifier measures the same construct everywhere it reads.

Research output

Ikram, A. and Haig, E. "Is the Platform Part of the Measurement? A Protocol and Simulation Study for Cross-Platform Equivalence of Machine-Labelled Policy Sentiment." Manuscript, complete; not published and not submitted. Target venue ICWSM 2027, Round 3 (15 January 2027). Treats each social platform as a survey mode and applies generalizability theory, measurement invariance and differential item functioning, and score linking to machine-labelled text. In a 500-replication simulation with known ground truth, an index that pools platform-level scores at face value reports the wrong sign of the aggregate on roughly a quarter of days, purely from measurement differences between platforms.

No peer-reviewed publications to date. The manuscript above is the only research output and I describe it exactly as it stands.
